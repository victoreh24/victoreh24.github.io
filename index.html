Services

What I do

For owners who need a system built, running and used by their team, and for teams hiring an engineer who has done it before.

  • Put your company on Odoo

    Sales, inventory, invoicing, accounting, point of sale and payroll in one system, set up around how you actually work: configuration, migration from your old system or spreadsheets, Hacienda electronic invoicing, training and manuals in Spanish or English. Odoo Community, self-hosted: no license fees, and you own everything.

  • Fix or extend the Odoo you already have

    An old version, a half-finished implementation, a module nobody supports, invoices Hacienda rejects. I have worked on Odoo since version 13 and cleaned up systems other people started, with a written fix list before any work begins.

  • Connect your systems so nobody re-types anything

    Card payments (TiloPay), WhatsApp, your phone system, your online store, supplier catalogs, your accountant's tools. If it has an API, or only a spreadsheet, it can talk to your ERP.

  • AI agents that answer the phone, the chat and your own questions

    A voice agent on your real phone line, a website chat that hands off to a person when it should, or an assistant inside Odoo that answers questions from live data. Measured before and after, and I will tell you when it is not worth it.

  • Apps and websites on the same back end

    A customer app or website that reads and writes your ERP: bookings, orders, loyalty, documents, payments. Design included: I started as a graphic designer.

  • Hiring for a remote engineering role?

    Forward-deployed, solutions or senior full-stack engineer. Native-level English and Spanish, UTC-6, a valid US visa for on-site visits.

How we'd work

Four steps, the same for every project. You know the scope before we build, and you own everything at the end.

  1. Step 1

    Discovery

    I sit with you and the people who do the work and follow an order, a delivery, an invoice and a payment through your company as they happen today. You get a plain-language map of what the system must do, in your words. If Odoo is the wrong answer, I say so here.

  2. Step 2

    Proposal

    A written scope, a price and a calendar: what gets configured, what gets built, what gets migrated, and who on your team owns each piece. Changes after that go on a short written note with their cost.

  3. Step 3

    Build

    A demo every week on your real data, not slides. Your team starts using pieces before the full go-live. User manuals in Spanish or English, and a direct line to me during the whole project.

  4. Step 4

    Handover

    Go-live on a date we choose together, with me on call that week, and training for each role. Then a support plan or hourly help. The server, the code and the data are yours.

About

Engineer, operator, marketer

I've been a web developer, a head of marketing, a hotel general manager, a CTO and, from late 2024 to 2026, head of technology and operations for an incubator and its portfolio companies, running 22 Odoo instances and a team of five.

Today I'm the lead engineer (and marketing manager) for a US-facing online gaming operator: I built its outreach platform, turned it into a multi-tenant SaaS, and put AI voice and chat agents on live phone lines with the guardrails that work requires.

I work AI-first: Claude Code multi-agent workflows to build and review; test suites, security reviews and staged deploys to decide what ships. At the incubator that cut module delivery from about 40 hours to about 5. I choose models and architectures by measuring, and I've shelved my own work when the numbers said so.

I also run businesses on Odoo myself (a hot-dog shop, a bakery and a tourism concierge), so when a cashier can't find the combo button, I hear about it at dinner.

Native-level English and Spanish. Based in San José, Costa Rica (UTC-6), open to remote work.
