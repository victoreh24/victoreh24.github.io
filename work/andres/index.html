An Odoo eCommerce store for a jewelry artist, with a course launch priced in USD

What it is

A handmade-jewelry store on Odoo 19 eCommerce for a Costa Rican artist. For a course launch I converted the store to USD with a tax-included price override, dated pricelist rules and a daily central-bank exchange-rate module, automated the course-access email, fixed the mail alias and a dead scheduler, and verified the launch with a 17-point checklist. I also ported the TiloPay card-payment provider and tested it against TiloPay's live API; it stays unpublished until the store has its own merchant account.

Highlights

  • USD store with tax-included pricing and a daily exchange-rate module
  • TiloPay provider ported and tested against the live API
